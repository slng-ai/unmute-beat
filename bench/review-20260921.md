# Review of 2026-09-21: what it found, and what changed

`POC_BENCHMARK_REVIEW.md` reviewed commit `28516da` and reproduced defects in
verification, business completion, stale offers, idempotency, cancellation and
measurement. This is the revision-specific ledger it asked for. It does **not**
rewrite `baseline/`: those records are what was true when they were written,
and several of them overstate what is covered today. Read them together.

Everything below was closed offline, against the same scripted core the tests
use. Nothing here was measured on a live call, and none of it settles whether
this harness is faster than the native arm -- §5 of the review is a protocol
for two arms, and one of the two is not in this repository.

## Deliberately unchanged

* **The Spain-only phone policy.** The reference conversation uses
  `+14155550199` and `open_call` raises on it, so a literal replay of §5.2
  cannot start. Widening the validator would undo a fix made after a real
  call; normalising both arms onto one Spanish identity is a scope decision,
  not a bug fix. **This one needs a decision before the paired runs.**
* **`SMART_TURN_STOP_SECS`.** 1.5s, and the trace shows it costs exactly that
  on utterances the analyser calls incomplete. Lowering it needs a recording
  of what it cuts off.
* **The global `invalid-argument-type` suppression.** Measured with the rule
  on: 31 diagnostics, not the ~15 the note claimed, and five of them are
  inside the SDK where ty resolves an explicit `Agent[SalonCall, object]` as
  `Agent[object, str]` anyway. Making `Node` generic -- the documented upgrade
  path -- would not clear those five, so the line cannot come off with it.
  Both notes now say the measured number.
* **The shared-history / repeated-prompt workaround.** Documented in
  `voice/core.py` where it is done. The `messages`/`reset`/owner-restoration
  contract is a design change, not a defect fix, and nothing here claims
  portable typed conversation behaviour.

## Still open, and what it needs

* **Latency parity.** Unproven, in either direction. It needs the paired,
  interleaved runs of §5.3 against a frozen native arm, and the native arm's
  build is not identified by its trace.
* **The prompt-level changes of 28516da** -- the inlined documents, the "same
  time" rule -- and whether a real provider answers in prose once `speak_by`
  withdraws its tools. All three need one live call.
* **Interruption on disconnect, and between turns.** Before, during and after
  a tool are covered, and during fixed speech is covered; the other two are
  not.
* **`tests/fixtures/trace_native.json`** is the reference call, trimmed to the
  fields this reader reads. If the native arm is rebuilt, this fixture is of
  the old one.
