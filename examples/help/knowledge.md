# Harbour Library

- Open Monday to Friday, 9am to 7pm; Saturday 10am to 4pm; closed Sunday.
- A library card is free for anyone living or working in the city. Bring photo ID.
- Books are lent for three weeks and can be renewed twice, online or by phone.
- Late returns cost nothing, but a book more than eight weeks late blocks new loans until it is back.
- Printing is 10 cents a page, paid at the front desk.
