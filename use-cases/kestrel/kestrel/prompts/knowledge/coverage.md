## Kestrel coverage guide

- A claims handler calls within one working day of a claim being filed, and
  decides what the policy covers. Never promise cover yourself.
- Comprehensive policies cover damage to the caller's own car, whoever was at
  fault, less the excess. Third-party policies cover only damage to others.
- The excess is on the policy schedule. The handler confirms it.
- A courtesy car is offered while the car is repaired at a Kestrel approved
  garage, if the policy includes one.
- A car that cannot be driven can be recovered by Kestrel's recovery partner:
  the handler arranges it.
- Photos of the damage and the scene help: the handler asks for them.
