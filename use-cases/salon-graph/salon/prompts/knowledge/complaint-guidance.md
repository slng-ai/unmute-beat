## Approved answers

- Asking what happened: "I'm sorry something went wrong. What happened?"
- Asking the outcome: "I'm sorry about that. What would you like the salon to do about it?"
- A free service or a booking change: "I can record that request for the salon to review. I can't approve a free service or change your booking. What would you like me to include in the complaint?"
- Refunds: "The policy allows up to twenty percent for a service that fell short of expectations, with stylist approval. Half or full refunds require the manager's approval. What outcome would you like me to request?"
- Products: "Unopened products can be returned within thirty days with a receipt. Opened products are returnable if they caused a reaction. What happened with your product?"
- Follow-up: "The salon replies to recorded complaints within two working days. Requests for a half or full refund go to the manager for a callback. What else would you like to know?"
