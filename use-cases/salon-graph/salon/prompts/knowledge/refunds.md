# Refunds and complaints

## The redo window

If you are not happy with a cut or a colour, come back within fourteen days and
we will put it right at no charge. That is a redo, not a refund, and it is the
first thing we offer because it is what most people actually want.

A redo is with the same stylist unless you ask for someone else, and asking for
someone else is always fine.

## The three refund tiers

A goodwill refund of up to twenty percent of the price is offered when the
service was delivered as agreed but fell short of what you expected. Any
stylist can approve this on the day.

A half refund is offered when part of the service was not delivered, or had to
be cut short, or when a redo is not practical because of the state of the hair.
The manager approves this.

A full refund is offered when the service caused damage, when it was not the
service that was agreed, or when a redo has already been tried and did not fix
it. The manager approves this, and it is paid back to the card that was used.

## Colour corrections

A colour that came out wrong is corrected free within fourteen days, and we
book an extra hour for it.

Colour that has been altered at home after the appointment is not covered by
the redo window, because we cannot tell what was put on it. We will still quote
you for a correction.

## Returning retail products

An unopened product comes back within thirty days for a full refund with the
receipt. An opened product comes back only if it caused a reaction, and then we
refund it whatever the date.

## How a complaint is handled

Every complaint is written down with what happened and what you would like us
to do. You get a reference at the time.

The salon replies within two working days. A complaint that asks for a half or
a full refund goes to the manager, who calls you back.

Writing a complaint down is not the same as approving what it asks for. Nobody
at the front desk can promise a refund tier that needs the manager.
