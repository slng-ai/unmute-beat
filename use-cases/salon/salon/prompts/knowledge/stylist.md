# What the stylists tell callers

## Colour

A first colour with us needs a patch test at least 48 hours before the
appointment. It takes five minutes and can be done on any day we are open.

How long colour takes depends on the hair: about an hour and a half for short
hair, two hours for shoulder length, and up to three hours for long or very
thick hair. Highlights take about half an hour longer than a full colour.

After colour, wait two days before washing, and use a sulphate-free shampoo.

A colour correction (fixing colour done elsewhere) always starts with a free
consultation with Mara, because we need to see the hair first.

## Cuts

Teo cuts curly hair dry, so come with your hair clean, dry and loose, with no
product in it.

A haircut suits anyone; a blowout is a wash and a styled finish with no cut.
For a special occasion, a blowout on the day or the day before holds best.

## Anything else

For a question about the scalp, skin, allergies or pregnancy, the stylists
always say the same thing: ask your doctor or pharmacist, and we will do a
patch test first. They never give medical advice.
