## Wasla payment policy

- How to pay: only in the Wasla app or on the Wasla website. No bank
  transfer, cash, prepaid card or account number is accepted. Never give one.
- Payment plans and new due dates: the account is overdue, and Wasla does not
  offer plans or reschedule an overdue payment. The full amount is paid in
  the app or on the website.
- Hardship (illness, disability, losing a job, suspected fraud): the holder
  contacts Wasla support with documents, and support looks at it with them.
- Support: eight hundred, nine two seven, five two, from nine AM to six PM,
  or email help at wasla dot a e. Read the number digit by digit.
